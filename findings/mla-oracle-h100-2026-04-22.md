# M6a — H100 bf16 oracle verdict (2026-04-22)

Phase M / M6a executed artifact. Second live GPU datapoint for the MLA
decode oracle paper, complementing M7 (Trillium bf16). Ran the PyTorch
CUDA port of the numpy FP32 reference on RunPod's `prism` pod (H100
SXM, SM90 Hopper) and graded the bf16 output against the committed
FP32 golden via the oracle at the `bf16` tolerance preset.

This is **not** M6 proper: M6 is the three Blackwell (SM100) bug
reproducers, which require B200 capacity and are still deferred. M6a
is a Hopper-control datapoint: does the absorbed-form MLA decode
algebra reproduce on a generation-older substrate? Yes, bit-level
consistent with the Trillium result.

## TL;DR

**PASS.** H100 bf16 against the FP32 reference: `cos_sim = 0.999984`,
`max_abs_diff = 6.85 × 10^{-3}`, `max_rel_diff = 4.72 × 10^{-3}`. All
three well inside the bf16 preset (max_abs 5e-2, max_rel 2e-2,
min_cos_sim 0.999). Tighter than M7 Trillium, which makes sense: H100
bf16 and our numpy reference both run in CPython+numpy/torch while
Trillium routes through JAX+XLA compilation to v6e — one fewer
compiler pass between the algorithm and the silicon.

## The real punchline: cross-substrate consistency

| Rail | Substrate | dtype | cos_sim vs FP32 ref |
|---|---|---|---|
| M7 | GCP Trillium v6e-1 | bf16 | 0.999977 |
| M6a | RunPod H100 SXM     | bf16 | 0.999984 |
| Reference | numpy FP32       | fp32 | 1.000000 |

The two bf16 substrates are within **7 × 10⁻⁶** of each other in
cosine similarity against the FP32 reference, on the seeded
committed inputs. This is **suggestive** evidence that the
absorbed-form decode algebra is substrate-consistent at bf16
precision for these inputs — the same algorithm, compiled through
two unrelated toolchains (CUDA on SM90 vs XLA/JAX on v6e), produces
closely-agreeing outputs.

This does **not** by itself establish that the three target
Blackwell NVFP4 bugs (SGLang #10284, vLLM #38439, FlashInfer #3047)
are kernel-local rather than spec-level. Neither bf16 rail
exercises SM100-only NVFP4 microscaling, TMEM / cluster-DSMEM
semantics, or trtllm-gen cubin dispatch — so a spec-level
interaction that only manifests at the NVFP4 layer would not show
up in either M6a or M7. The kernel-local attribution for the three
target bugs remains **provisional** pending M6b (B200 bug
reproducer execution under NVFP4 / FP8 against the oracle on real
SM100 hardware).
