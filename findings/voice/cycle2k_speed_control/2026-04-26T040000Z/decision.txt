# Why decision is held INCONCLUSIVE rather than auto-deployed
1. Agent cannot listen. The audible-tag failure mode (K1 §Anticipated-
   failure-mode) is not auto-detectable: a tag like "[urgent dispatcher
   pace]" might be SPOKEN as words by Fish S2-Pro instead of consumed as
   voice direction. wpm metric alone cannot distinguish "fast natural
   speech" from "fast speech that begins with the literal phrase 'urgent
   dispatcher pace'." Listener must confirm bracket tokens are silent.
2. Voice-identity drift risk. A tag may shift the speaker character
   (different person sounding voice). Detectable only by ear.
3. Comma cadence on P1. Even at 193.5 wpm the comma may still produce
   an awkward micro-pause in T1; user may prefer T5 if the pause sounds
   more natural at higher pace.
