## Listening rubric (per phrase, mark winner)

For each phrase, listen blind A/B and rate:

- **Authority** — does the voice command attention without sounding panicked?
- **Calmness** — is prosody steady, no rising panic intonation?
- **Clarity** — every word crisp, no slur or mumble?
- **Speed** — appropriate pace for caller comprehension under stress?
